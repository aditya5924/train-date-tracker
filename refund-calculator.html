How are IRCTC Cancellation Charges Calculated?

When you cancel an Indian Railways train ticket booked via IRCTC, the system does not process a flat 100% refund. The deduction (also known as a cancellation fee or clerkage charge) is heavily dependent on two factors: the current status of your ticket, and when you cancel it relative to the train's scheduled departure time.

1. Confirmed Ticket Cancellation Rules

If you possess a fully confirmed ticket, timing is everything. The IRCTC uses a tiered penalty system:

  • 48 Hours Before Departure: A flat cancellation charge is applied based on your travel class (e.g., ₹120 for Sleeper, ₹180 for 3AC).
  • 48 Hours to 12 Hours Before Departure: The railway deducts 25% of the base fare, subject to the minimum flat rate of your travel class.
  • 12 Hours to 4 Hours Before Departure: The deduction increases to 50% of the base fare.
  • Less than 4 Hours (Or Chart Preparation): No refund is granted for confirmed tickets.

2. RAC and Waitlisted (WL) Ticket Refunds

Passengers holding Reservation Against Cancellation (RAC) or Waitlisted tickets face much lighter penalties. If the ticket is cancelled up to 30 minutes before the scheduled departure of the train, IRCTC only deducts a nominal clerkage charge of ₹60 per passenger (plus GST for AC classes). The remaining balance is automatically refunded to the original payment source.

3. Tatkal Ticket Refund Policy

The Tatkal quota is meant for emergency travel. Because of this, IRCTC grants zero refund upon the cancellation of a confirmed Tatkal ticket. However, if your Tatkal ticket remains waitlisted or RAC, the standard ₹60 clerkage deduction applies, and the rest is refunded.
