About Train Date Tracker

Welcome to the Train Date Tracker, your ultimate utility for calculating the exact advance reservation period (ARP) for Indian Railways journeys.

Our Mission

Securing a confirmed train ticket on the IRCTC network is highly competitive, especially during major festivals, holidays, and peak seasons. Because the advance booking window is strictly set to 60 days, passengers often struggle to manually calculate the exact opening date due to varying month lengths and leap years. Our mission is to eliminate that confusion by providing a fast, ad-free, and mathematically flawless calculator that instantly gives you the exact date your booking window opens.

Who Built This?

The Train Date Tracker was conceptualized and developed by Aditya Mahajan, a dedicated Computer Science (BSc CS) student. Driven by a passion for full-stack web development and solving real-world digital friction, Aditya built this platform to provide a seamless, highly responsive utility for millions of daily train commuters across India.
