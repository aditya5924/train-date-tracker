What is Tatkal Booking?

The Tatkal scheme is an emergency ticketing quota managed by Indian Railways for passengers who need to travel at short notice. Because availability is severely limited, tickets often sell out within 60 to 90 seconds.

Tatkal booking always opens one day in advance of the actual date of journey from the train's originating station.

5 Expert Tricks for Faster Tatkal Checkout

1. The "Master List" Hack

Log into your IRCTC account before 9:45 AM, go to My Profile > Add/Modify Master List. Save all passenger details beforehand. When booking opens, simply click "Fetch from Master List" to instantly auto-fill the forms.

2. Login Timing Strategy

The golden window is 9:57 AM to 9:58 AM. Log in, keep your mouse over the "Book Now" button, and hit refresh at exactly 09:59:58 AM.

3. Payment Gateway Optimization

Use the IRCTC eWallet or UPI (Google Pay / PhonePe) for instant payments to avoid OTP delays from debit/credit cards.

Premium Tatkal (PT) vs Normal Tatkal:
Premium Tatkal operates on dynamic pricing. As seats fill up, the price increases. If money is not an issue, search for PT availability first, as they stay available longer.
