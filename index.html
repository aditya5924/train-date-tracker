About This Site

Welcome to the Train Date Tracker.

Indian Railways (IRCTC) allows passengers to book train tickets up to 60 days in advance. Calculating this exact date manually can be confusing.

This website serves as a simple utility to help you instantly calculate the exact date your ticket booking window opens, ensuring you never miss the chance to secure a confirmed seat.
