Cancellation & Refund Guide

  • Confirmed Tatkal Tickets: No refund is granted (Zero refund) upon cancellation.
  • Waitlisted / RAC Tatkal: If cancelled before chart preparation, basic clerkage charges (₹60 + GST per passenger) are deducted and the remaining amount is refunded.

If you cancel the ticket at least 48 hours before the scheduled departure, the flat deduction per passenger is:
  • AC 1st Class / Executive: ₹240 + GST
  • AC 2 Tier / 1st Class: ₹200 + GST
  • AC 3 Tier / Chair Car: ₹180 + GST
  • Sleeper Class: ₹120
  • Second Class: ₹60
Note: If cancelled less than 48 hours before departure, cancellation charges can increase to 25% or 50% of the ticket fare depending on the exact timing.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does IRCTC advance ticket booking open?

Advance ticket booking typically opens at 08:00 AM IST sharp on the day your booking window opens (60 days prior to the journey date).

Does the 60-day window include the journey date?

No, the advance reservation period is calculated backwards starting from the day before your journey date.

Why should I add reminders to my calendar?

Popular trains sell out within minutes during peak holiday seasons. Setting a calendar reminder ensures you are logged in and ready right at 8:00 AM.

About Train Date Tracker

Welcome to Train Date Tracker, an independent utility web application designed to simplify railway travel planning.

Why Use This Calculator?

Indian Railways (IRCTC) permits passengers to book reserved train tickets up to 60 days in advance (excluding the date of journey itself). Manually calculating this date—especially when factoring in varying month lengths, leap years, and holiday booking rushes—can lead to missed booking windows and unconfirmed tickets.

Key Features

  • Instant 60-Day Window Calculation: Automatically figures out the exact date and time your booking opens.
  • Festival & Holiday Alerts: Automatically flags high-demand travel seasons like Holi, Diwali, Durga Puja, and Christmas.
  • Global Timezone Support: Automatically converts the 08:00 AM IST booking rush time into your local timezone if you are booking from abroad.
  • Calendar Integration: Generates instant `.ics` reminder files so you never miss a booking slot.
