Understanding Indian Railways Seating Layouts

Our Berth Calculator uses the official mathematical algorithms designed by the Indian Railways to map out the coach structures. Below is a detailed breakdown of how these coaches are designed.

1. Sleeper (SL) and 3-Tier AC (3A) Coaches

These are the most common overnight travel coaches. The mathematical seating pattern repeats in blocks of 8 seats:

  • Lower Berth (LB): Seat numbers ending in 1 and 4.
  • Middle Berth (MB): Seat numbers ending in 2 and 5.
  • Upper Berth (UB): Seat numbers ending in 3 and 6.
  • Side Lower (SL): Seat numbers ending in 7.
  • Side Upper (SU): Seat numbers ending in 8.
Why is there no 1AC in the calculator?
Indian Railways does not assign specific seat numbers at the time of booking for 1AC. The seats are allocated manually during chart preparation.
